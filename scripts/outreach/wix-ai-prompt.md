# Wix AI (Gemini) prompt — mask12.com wholesale announcement campaign

How to use:
1. Wix Dashboard → Email Marketing → Create campaign → AI assistant.
2. Paste the PROMPT below.
3. For each segment list, run it once, swapping the single "Product-focus line".
4. After it generates: set the greeting merge tag to {First Name} with fallback
   "there", confirm the unsubscribe + mailing address are in the footer (CASL),
   send a test to yourself, then send to that segment's label.

---

## PROMPT (paste into Wix AI)

Create a B2B wholesale email campaign announcing our new online wholesale catalog.

BUSINESS: Butterfly Fashion Trading — a wholesale supplier in Toronto (North York, ON), serving Canadian retailers since 1996. We sell variety, novelty, seasonal and impulse goods in bulk: fidget/squishy & novelty toys, winter accessories (gloves, hats, masks), gift & fashion accessories, smoke-shop supplies, and World Cup 2026 fan gear.

WEBSITE TO PROMOTE: mask12.com — our full wholesale catalog, online for the first time.

AUDIENCE: our existing and past customers — independent retailers across Canada (dollar & variety stores, gift shops, convenience stores, toy shops, pharmacies). This is a WARM re-engagement to people who have ordered from or contacted us before — NOT a cold sales pitch. Write like a real supplier emailing a returning customer.

GOAL / PRIMARY CTA: get them to visit mask12.com and create a free B2B account (approved within 24 hours) to see wholesale pricing and order online.

KEY POINTS (as short scannable bullets):
- Our full catalog is now online at mask12.com
- Real inventory stocked in Toronto — ships across Canada & the USA
- No minimum order — order any quantity
- Case pricing and fast, easy reorders for approved B2B accounts
- Free B2B account, usually approved within 24 hours

TONE: warm, professional, concise, friendly B2B. Plain and confident, no hype, no exclamation-heavy marketing voice.

STRUCTURE:
- Subject line: include "Butterfly Fashion" and that the wholesale catalog is now online at mask12.com; under 60 characters. Also give a short preheader/preview line.
- Greeting using the contact's first name with a fallback to "there".
- One short intro sentence (we've supplied Canadian retailers since 1996; our full catalog is now online).
- The bullet list above.
- One product-focus line (see below).
- A single clear call-to-action button: "Browse the wholesale catalog" linking to https://mask12.com, and mention creating a free B2B account.
- Sign-off from "James, Butterfly Fashion Trading", phone 416-785-5999, mask12.com.
- CASL-compliant footer: mailing address "Butterfly Fashion Trading, 178 Bentworth Ave, North York, ON M6A 1P7", a line saying they're receiving this because they've ordered from or contacted us, and a working unsubscribe link.

CONSTRAINTS: body under ~150 words, mobile-friendly, one primary CTA button, clean and trustworthy wholesale look.

PRODUCT-FOCUS LINE FOR THIS CAMPAIGN (use exactly one):
"New for your shelves: novelty gifts, fashion accessories, and seasonal décor — by the case."

Also give me 3 alternative subject lines to choose from.
